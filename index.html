About Bipity Bopity Cakes

Once Upon a Cake…

Every sweet story has a beginning, and mine began with a little flour, a dash of creativity, and a whole lot of love. Welcome to Bipity Bopity Cakes, I’m Amanda, your Fairy Cake-Mother! Where ordinary celebrations are sprinkled with a little extra magic.

I’m the baker, dreamer, and creator behind the magic. What began as a love for baking and creating beautiful treats blossomed into a passion for turning celebrations into something truly unforgettable.

I believe every special moment deserves its own happily-ever-after and sometimes, that happily-ever-after comes with buttercream, sprinkles, and a beautifully decorated cake.

From the first swirl of frosting to the tiniest finishing detail, I pour my heart into every creation. I love taking your ideas and dreams, and your sweetest celebrations and turning them into edible works of art tailored just for you.

At Bipity Bopity Cakes, every cake has a story, every celebration has a little magic, and every order is made with love.

So come on in, make a wish, and let’s create something sweet…

Because sometimes, all it takes is a little bit of magic to turn a moment into a memory.

And they baked happily ever after…

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

Bipity Bopity Cakes brings a sprinkle of sweetness and a touch of fairytale magic to Lodi, Stockton, Galt, Lockeford, and the surrounding corners of San Joaquin County.

Does Bipity Bopity Cakes deliver in Stockton, CA?

Yes, a little bakery magic can come right to your doorstep! Delivery is available for an additional fee, with the final cost determined by the mileage and time. Let us bring a touch of enchantment straight to your celebration!

Do you make custom themed birthday cakes?

Absolutely! At Bipity Bopity Cakes, Amanda brings fairy-tale dreams to life with custom-decorated cakes and cupcakes for life’s most enchanting celebrations. From birthdays filled with wonder and whimsical novelty or pop-culture-inspired designs to magical weddings, romantic engagements, joyful gender reveals, and holiday festivities, every creation is sprinkled with a little extra magic.

How far in advance should I order a custom cake?

To ensure every sweet creation is crafted with a little extra magic, please place your orders 3–4 weeks in advance. If your special occasion is arriving sooner, kindly reach out, and we’ll see if a little fairytale magic can make your wishes come true!

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

At this time, our enchanted bakery is unable to accommodate vegan or gluten-free requests. Thank you for understanding as we continue crafting our magical sweet creations!

Do you make wedding or engagement cakes?

Of course! Amanda would be delighted to create a sweet masterpiece for your happily ever after. Whether you’re celebrating a birthday, saying “I do,” announcing a little prince or princess on the way, celebrating an engagement, or adding sweetness to your favorite holidays, each custom cake and cupcake is thoughtfully crafted to make your special moments feel like a real-life fairy tale.

Is there a storefront I can visit?

Bipity Bopity Cakes is a home-based bakery tucked away in our own little fairytale kingdom, without a public storefront or walk-in location. Each magical order is arranged individually, with pickup and delivery options available throughout the surrounding San Joaquin County area.

Service Area & Contact
